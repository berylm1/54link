DEPLOYMENT MODEL

Phase 1 — Dev environments on 54link infrastructure (Kubernetes), wired to government-agency sandboxes (NCS B’Odogwu, CBN TMS, NEPC, NIS). Phase 2 — Video walkthroughs published per platform as deployments ship. Phase 3 — Staging → production for Nigerian agency partners. Phase 4 — Replicate the playbook across additional African markets.

PARTNERSHIP MODELS
