54link — Platform Brochure (DRAFT v0.1)
========================================

54link compiles, deploys, and showcases Africa's operational platforms.
Nigeria is the first use case; the blueprint scales across all 54 countries.

THE PORTFOLIO
-------------
34 platforms compiled from 66 public repositories:

 1. NDSEP / NGApp — National Data Sovereignty Enforcement Platform (NDPA 2023)
 2. Meridian TaxTech — Nigerian NRS unified tax platform (compliance, gov enclave, inclusion)
 3. Blue Economy Platform — Nigerian maritime PPP (24 services: tracking, insurance, ticketing, traceability)
 4. UmojaFlowOS — Cross-border B2B payment orchestration (NGN/KES/ZAR)
 5. AgricPlatform — Nigeria Farmer Platform (NYFN PRD v3.3)
 6. Nexcomm Exchange — African agricultural commodity exchange
 7. BIS — AML/CFT compliance intelligence
 8. Unified SOS — Subnational digital governance & revenue (6 pilot states)
 9. VPP — Open-source virtual power plant
10. FraudFusion — Multi-service fraud prevention
11. NextGen INSR — Nigerian insurtech (full value chain)
12. Payment Switch Onboarding (paygate / paymentswitch / crmaas)
13. RemitFlow — Cross-border remittance microservices
14. TaxStamp — Excise stamp issuance & verification
15. TourismPay — Multi-currency tourism payments
16. HealthLink — Healthcare data linkage
17. CRUSH — Healthcare fraud/waste/abuse operations
18. H2Fleet — Hydrogen bus fleet platform
19. INEC — Election management system
20. MineralVision — Mineral exploration & compliance
21. Lanai — Luxury travel concierge
22. Agora — Open-source AI receptionist SaaS
23. Singlewindow — Trade facilitation portal
24. Healthpoint — Healthcare data security (ML)
... plus 11 additional repositories in the portfolio.

CORE STACK
----------
Go · Python · Rust · TypeScript
Postgres (pgvector) · Redis/Dragonfly · Kafka · Temporal · Keycloak
TigerBeetle ledger · Mojaloop FSPIOP · APISIX · OpenAppSec WAF
Docker · Kubernetes (hybrid GitOps) · GitHub Actions CI/CD

DEPLOYMENT MODEL
----------------
Phase 1 — Dev environments on 54link infrastructure (Kubernetes), wired to
          government-agency sandboxes (NCS B'Odogwu, CBN TMS, NEPC, NIS).
Phase 2 — Video walkthroughs published per platform as deployments ship.
Phase 3 — Staging → production for Nigerian agency partners.
Phase 4 — Replicate the playbook across additional African markets.

PARTNERSHIP MODELS
------------------
- Government: sovereign deployments, agency sandbox integration, capacity building
- Enterprise: platform licensing, integration engineering, managed operations
- Developers: open-source contribution, ecosystem tooling, paid build work
