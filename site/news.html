Nigeria launches $300m distributed renewable energy fund as World Bank commits $25m

The Nigeria DRE Fund, co-managed by NSIA and Africa50 with SEforALL, has reached commercial operation, moving from structuring to active capital deployment for mini-grids, solar home systems and storage. AU commissioner: Africa needs $64bn a year and cross-border power trade to close energy gap

African Union Infrastructure and Energy Commissioner Lerato Dorothy Mataboge told the Global Africa Business Initiative in New York that closing Africa electricity access gap, with roughly 600 million people unserved, requires about $64 billion annually.
