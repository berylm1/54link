DRC-UAE trade agreement takes effect, opening duty-free market access

The Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement between the DR Congo and the UAE entered into force on October 30, granting 6,000 Congolese products duty-free access to the Emirati market and covering services, investment, procurement and digital commerce. AfDB to co-lead Electrify Now Finance Coalition unveiled at COP31

At COP31 in Antalya, a new Electrify Now Finance Coalition was announced to mobilise governments, development banks and private investors behind electrification in emerging and developing economies, with the African Development Bank and World Bank co-chairing from 2027. The coalition backs COP31's 35-by-35 Global Electrification Pledge, which aims to lift electricity's share of global final energy consumption from about 23% to 35% by 2035. Sustainable Energy for All CEO Damilola Ogunbiyi framed electrification as a route to industrialisation, jobs and energy independence for Africa.

QatarEnergy to take 25% of Chevron-operated PEL 82 offshore Namibia

QatarEnergy agreed to acquire a 25% participating interest in Petroleum Exploration License 82, an offshore oil exploration licence in the Walvis Basin, from a Chevron affiliate. Chevron remains operator with 55% after completion, while Namcor E&P and Custos Energy each retain 10%. The licence covers blocks 2112B and 2212A across roughly 11,444 sq km in water depths of 350 to 2,300 metres, and deepens QatarEnergy's growing footprint in one of Africa's hottest exploration frontiers.
