World Bank: behind-the-border barriers, not tariffs, are the biggest brake on African trade

A new World Bank report, Integrating Africa from Threads to Hubs, finds about 60% of Africa's trade costs come from unilateral behind-the-border barriers such as customs inefficiencies, unaligned standards and poor infrastructure, rather than tariffs. Only 15-17% of Africa's exports stay within the continent, and intra-regional trade intensity has declined since 2018. The report urges AfCFTA to move from legal commitments to functional integration, saying deeper trade agreements could raise exports by up to 56%.
