  • KYC verification and document management
  • Workflow designer (15 workflows, 139 steps)
  • Event streaming: Kafka, Fluvio, Dapr
  • Temporal orchestration with retry policies
  • Keycloak identity + Permify authorisation
  • Delta Lake / Spark / Flink lakehouse
  • Geospatial analytics with Apache Sedona
  • AI insights and advanced reporting

Problem it solves

Social protection programmes disburse to millions of beneficiaries through fragmented systems, which invites leakage, duplicate payments and untraceable grievances. This platform puts enrolment, verification, payment and grievance handling on one accountable, auditable spine — with the data platform underneath to detect fraud and measure delivery.
